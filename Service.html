Our Comprehensive Services

We offer end-to-end solutions with a focus on precision, compliance, and minimal downtime. Our services adhere to international standards like IEC, ISO, and FDA guidelines.

Repair Services

Expert diagnostics and repair for MRI (1.5T–3T), CT scanners, X-ray systems using OEM parts. Includes coil testing, gradient amplifier repairs, and software updates. Precision to ±0.1% accuracy. 24/7 emergency response.

Maintenance

Preventive and corrective maintenance for ventilators, anesthesia machines, defibrillators per IEC 60601. Includes leak testing, battery cycling, and performance audits. Customized contracts ensure 99.9% uptime with predictive analytics.

Supplies

Wide range of probes, transducers, batteries, cables for ultrasound, endoscopy, radiology — FDA-compliant with full traceability. Inventory management and just-in-time delivery to reduce costs.

Specialized Dental Services

Expanding our expertise to dental equipment, we provide tailored services for clinics in Dubai and the UAE, ensuring compliance with dental-specific standards like EN 13060 for sterilization.

Dental Chair & Unit Repair

Hydraulic and electrical repairs for dental chairs (e.g., A-dec, Belmont). Includes upholstery replacement, motor calibration, and integration with delivery systems. Torque accuracy ±5% for handpiece mounts.

Dental Imaging Maintenance

Calibration and maintenance for intraoral X-rays, panoramic units, and CBCT scanners. Radiation safety checks, sensor cleaning, and software optimization for clear imaging at low doses.

Dental Sterilization & Supplies

Autoclave validation (Class B/S), handpiece lubrication, and supplies like burs, scalers, and intraoral cameras. Biological indicator testing and cycle logging for infection control compliance.

Technical Expertise Across Devices

  • Imaging Systems: MRI (1.5T-3T), CT (64-256 slices), X-ray (digital/analog), Dental CBCT (FOV up to 16x10 cm).
  • Life Support: Ventilators (invasive/non-invasive, flow rates up to 15L/min), Anesthesia machines.
  • Diagnostic: ECG (12-lead), Ultrasound (4D imaging), Lab analyzers (throughput 500 tests/hr), Intraoral cameras (HD resolution).
  • Dental Specific: Handpieces (speed up to 400,000 RPM), Autoclaves (steam pressure 2.1 bar), Scalers (ultrasonic frequency 28-32 kHz).
