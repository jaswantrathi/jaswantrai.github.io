FAQ
Answers to common questions

Your operations depend on clarity and reliability. Here are answers to the questions we're asked most often by biomedical and facility teams.

Ask us directly →
What is your emergency response time?
+
We typically respond within 4 hours in Dubai and 6–8 hours across other Emirates, depending on location and access. For critical life support equipment, we prioritize immediate dispatch.
Are your engineers certified and compliant?
+
Yes. Our engineers are OEM‑trained where applicable and operate under ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485 quality frameworks, fully aligned with MOH and DHA regulations.
Do you provide spare parts and OEM components?
+
We source and supply genuine OEM parts or approved equivalents, depending on your budget and regulatory requirements, and always document replacements for audit trails.
Can you support multi‑site hospital groups?
+
Absolutely. We work with single clinics and multi‑facility hospital networks, offering centralized reporting, unified SLAs, and standardized maintenance protocols.
What is the difference between AMC and CMC?
+
An AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) covers scheduled preventive maintenance and labour. A CMC (Comprehensive Maintenance Contract) also includes spare parts, extended warranty, and a dedicated account manager — ideal for multi-site facilities.
Do you cover all seven Emirates?
+
Yes. From our Bur Dubai headquarters we serve all UAE Emirates — with dedicated teams available in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and Al Ain for faster response times across the country.
