About ICON BIOTRONICS

Founded in Dubai, ICON BIOTRONICS is a premier biomedical and dental engineering firm specializing in the service, repair, maintenance, and supply of medical and dental equipment. With over 15 years of experience, our certified engineers handle everything from diagnostic imaging systems to dental units and sterilization devices.

We are committed to ISO 9001:2015, FDA, and CE standards, ensuring every service meets global quality benchmarks. Our team uses state-of-the-art tools for precise calibrations, electrical safety testing (IEC 60601), and performance verification, minimizing risks and maximizing efficiency.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, innovative solutions that enhance patient care and operational excellence in healthcare and dental facilities.

Technical Expertise

Our engineers are trained in advanced diagnostics, including signal processing for imaging equipment and torque calibration for dental handpieces. We specialize in:

  • Biomedical: MRI/CT field strength calibration (up to 3T), ventilator flow accuracy (±2%).
  • Dental: Chair hydraulics testing, X-ray radiation leakage checks (per IAEA standards), autoclave cycle validation.
  • Preventive Protocols: Risk-based maintenance using FMECA analysis for critical devices.
  • Training: In-house certification programs for staff on equipment handling and compliance.
