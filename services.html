Specialized Dental Services

Expanding our expertise to dental equipment, we provide tailored services for clinics in Dubai and the UAE, ensuring compliance with dental-specific standards like EN 13060 for sterilization and ADA guidelines.

Dental Chair & Delivery Systems

Hydraulic and electrical repairs for dental chairs (A-dec, Belmont, Pelton). Includes upholstery replacement, motor calibration, waterline disinfection, and integration with delivery systems. Torque accuracy ±5% for handpiece mounts. Suction system optimization for 95% debris removal.

  • Foot pedal responsiveness testing
  • Amalgam separator maintenance
  • LED light curing unit calibration

Dental Imaging & Radiology

Calibration and maintenance for intraoral X-rays, panoramic units, and CBCT scanners. Radiation safety checks per ICRP guidelines, sensor cleaning, and software optimization for clear imaging at low doses (≤20 mSv/year). DICOM compatibility ensured.

  • Patient dose monitoring

Autoclave validation (Class B/S), handpiece lubrication and balancing, and supplies like burs, scalers, and intraoral cameras. Biological indicator testing, cycle logging, and Bowie-Dick tests for infection control compliance. Handpiece speed up to 400,000 RPM with vibration <0.5μm.

  • Ultrasonic scaler frequency 28-32 kHz
