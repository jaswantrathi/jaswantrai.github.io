Who We Are

Engineering Excellence Since 2009

Founded in Bur Dubai, ICON BIOTRONICS began with one mission: give UAE hospitals access to world-class biomedical engineering without OEM price tags. Today we are one of the UAE's most trusted independent biomedical and dental engineering companies.

From a single-room workshop to a multi-engineer operation serving 500+ healthcare facilities across all seven Emirates — our growth is built on technical excellence and genuine care for patient safety.

ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 13485 certified quality management across all service lines
OEM-certified engineers trained directly by GE, Philips, Siemens, Dräger and more
DHA, MOH, HAAD and JCIA audit-ready documentation for every service
Genuine 24/7 emergency response — a live engineer, not an answering service
Transparent pricing — 30–45% savings vs OEM contracts, guaranteed in writing
